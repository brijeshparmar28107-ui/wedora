    Smart Budget Tips

    Simple, practical advice to help you stay on budget.

    Keep venue expenses within a reasonable portion of your total budget — ideally 25–30%.

    Always keep an emergency fund of at least 10% for unexpected costs.

    Track every single expense, no matter how small — it adds up quickly.

    Compare at least three vendor prices before booking any service.

    Review your budget regularly and adjust as plans evolve.

    Negotiate politely — many vendors offer flexible packages and discounts.
