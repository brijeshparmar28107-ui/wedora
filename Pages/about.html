    Our Story

    Built for Couples, Designed for Joy

    Wedora was created to make wedding planning easier. We saw how quickly the process could become overwhelming — with lists in one app, budgets in another, and vendor conversations scattered everywhere.

    So we brought the important planning activities into one organized platform, where couples can plan with clarity and enjoy the journey.
